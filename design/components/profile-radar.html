Profile radar

SVG shape of answered spectra inside Profile. Radius is commitment (|score|); only placed axes appear. Stance stroke on the polygon.

Documented

Rules

  • 0 placed → rings only.
  • 2 placed → .prof-poly-line between vertices.
  • 3+ placed → filled .prof-poly.
  • Vertex labels show the leaning pole name, not the axis title when committed.
  • Clicking a vertex scrolls to the matching .prof-axis in the product.
