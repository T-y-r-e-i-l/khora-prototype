Voice & tone

Calm, precise, slightly literary. Chrome is short and uppercase; thought is prose.

  • Chrome — micro labels: Explore, Reading, New note. No emoji.
  • Empty states — one serif headline, one short sentence, one action.
  • Insights — quote first, then reading; lineage as a quiet tag.
  • Errors / danger — rare; use .ghost.danger for irreversible acts.
