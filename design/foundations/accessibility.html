Accessibility

Practical constraints of the dark glass UI — not a full WCAG audit.

  • Prefer visible :focus-within / :focus-visible on search and selects.
  • Don’t rely on orb color alone — pair with labels or caps text.
  • Tooltips (.read-tip) are supplemental; keep critical copy in the UI.
  • Respect prefers-reduced-motion for field drift and orb liquid.
  • Touch targets on phone FAB and nav items stay ≥40px.
